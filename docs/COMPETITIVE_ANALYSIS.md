## 3. Market Opportunity

- **Indian algorithmic trading** market: Growing rapidly with SEBI's push for transparency. Algo execution now requires SEBI registration, creating a gap for "advisory + tool" platforms.
- **Retail participation** in Indian equities at an all-time high: Demat accounts crossed 150 million (2024–2025), up from 40 million in 2020.
- **LLM adoption** in Indian fintech growing: Zerodha, Groww, and others exploring AI but no dedicated multi-agent platform.
- **Gap identified:** No accessible multi-agent AI trading platform for Indian retail traders that combines research, risk, and execution.

---

## 4. Differentiation Strategy

1. **Explainability First** — Every trade shows exactly which agents said what, in plain English + Hindi
2. **Debate-Based Research** — Bull vs Bear agents create balanced, non-biased signals for Indian stocks
3. **Risk Manager Gate** — No trade executes without independent risk validation; checks SEBI circuit limits and margin
4. **Paper-to-Live Graduation** — Users must prove profitability in paper for 30 days before live auto-execute unlock
5. **Community Leaderboard** — Users can publish (anonymized) agent strategies and rank on Indian market performance
6. **Indian Market Context** — Agents understand F&O expiry, budget impacts, RBI policy days, quarterly result seasons
